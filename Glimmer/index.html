Our Story

A Legacy of Flavor & Passion

Born in the heart of Chicago's Gold Coast, Glimmer has been redefining the tapas experience for over four decades. We are more than a restaurant — we are a destination, a gathering place, a celebration of life through food, wine, and vibrant company.

Our chefs travel annually to Spain to bring back authentic traditions, reimagined with the finest local ingredients. Each small plate tells a story of culture, craft, and culinary devotion.
